# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks.
- No doxxing. Never post another person's real name, address, IP, or account details.
- No posting player data from servers, raw UUIDs, or recordings of players who did not agree to it.
- Keep cheat talk technical. Sharing cheat clients, configs or download links is not allowed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block anyone who breaks these rules.

To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.
